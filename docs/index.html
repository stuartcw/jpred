Think You Know the J.League? Prove It.

JPRED began with a simple question among a group of J.League fans:

Who’s going to finish where this season?

The rules were simple. Pick the top three and bottom three clubs in each league, post your predictions on Twitter with the #JPRED hashtag, then spend the rest of the season discovering just how wrong you were.

What started as a bit of friendly competition quickly took off. Predictions poured in from across the J.League community.

Then came the difficult part.

At the end of the season, JPRED organiser Ritchie bravely disappeared into Twitter search, hunted down every entry, compiled them into one enormous spreadsheet, and calculated the results by hand.

Thankfully, those days are over.

JPRED is now fully automated. Make your predictions, follow the season, and watch the standings change as the results come in.
